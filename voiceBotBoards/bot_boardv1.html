How these numbers are worked out
  • Visit booked: the lead has a branch visit scheduled date or a home visit scheduled date. Both present means "booked for both".
  • Visit done: a visit done date exists and is on or after the day of the scheduled visit. Same-day visits count because the done date has no time.
  • Visit done at: decided only by which done date has data, so a lead who booked branch but visited home shows as Home.
  • Stage after visit: the lead's current stage, only if the stage was updated on or after the visit done date. Otherwise it is counted as "No stage update after visit".
  • Admission: visited leads whose stage after the visit is Admission Done.
  • Conversion rates: booking rate is booked out of analysed, visit rate is done out of booked, admission rate is admitted out of visited, overall is admitted out of analysed.
